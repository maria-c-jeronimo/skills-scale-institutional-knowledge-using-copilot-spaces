# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Professional

### Role Summary
QA/Testing professionals ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and identify risks before release. They collaborate with developers, product teams, and project leads to define quality standards and execute test plans.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and acceptance test plans
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Identify and track quality issues and regressions
- Participate in sprint planning and definition of done discussions
- Report quality metrics and risk signals

### Goals
- Ensure customer satisfaction through reliable, high-quality releases
- Reduce production incidents and rework
- Provide early visibility into quality risks

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test plan documentation and status updates
- Bug reports and quality dashboards
- Collaboration with developers on edge cases and test coverage

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation quality, reproduce defects, and confirm fixes.
- Partners with Product Managers on acceptance criteria, user impact, and release readiness.
- Informs Project Managers about quality risks, blockers, and readiness for milestones or releases.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads make architectural decisions, guide technical implementation, and ensure solutions align with system design principles and scalability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture
- Review design proposals and code architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on best practices
- Ensure technical quality and maintainability
- Collaborate with product on feasibility trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Enable fast, confident delivery

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review participation and guidance
- Risk and dependency escalations
- Estimation and planning collaboration

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by clarifying technical standards, architectural constraints, and implementation guidance.
- Works with Product Managers to assess feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery risk before committing to scope.
- Helps Project Managers understand dependencies, risks, and sequencing needed for successful execution.
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on non-functional requirements, testability, and release confidence.

---

## Design/UX Professional

### Role Summary
Design and UX professionals define user experiences, create design specifications, and advocate for usability and accessibility in product delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define information architecture
- Create wireframes, mockups, and design specifications
- Collaborate with product on feature prioritization and trade-offs
- Validate designs with users and stakeholders
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards
- Provide design guidance and review during development

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Build products customers love using

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design rationale
- Design specs and component documentation
- Design reviews with product and engineering
- Usability testing and feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate customer needs into clear experience goals and feature direction.
- Works closely with Developers to ensure implementation matches the intended user experience and accessibility requirements.
- Shares validation findings with QA/Testing to support usability and acceptance testing.
- Provides design context to Project Managers for milestone planning and stakeholder communication.

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, decision-making authority, and resource support for projects. They represent business, customer, or organizational interests.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Make prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Allocate resources and remove blockers
- Provide input on customer needs and market context
- Approve major releases and decisions
- Escalate risks and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment across organizational priorities
- Manage risks and dependencies at the sponsor level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Decision reviews and approval gates
- Escalation notifications
- Release announcements and outcome tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Sets priorities and confirms business direction alongside Product Managers.
- Relies on Project Managers for planning, status visibility, and decision support.
- Reviews major milestones and release readiness with technical and delivery leads.
- Provides escalation support when cross-team dependency or resourcing issues require sponsor attention.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a clear operating model for delivery: Product defines the outcome, Project manages the plan, Developers implement it, QA validates it, Design shapes the experience, Technical Leads guide the architecture, and Stakeholders provide business direction and support.
